HVV Crowd Prediction

Smarter Decisions for a Dynamic Hamburg

Our Vision

The streets of Hamburg are full of life — but that also means congestion, unpredictability, and missed opportunities for businesses. Our vision was simple: use data to make the city smarter. We set out to build a system that predicts when and where people gather, so both citizens and entrepreneurs can plan better.

Why This Project Matters

Every day, small business owners, food trucks, and new startups struggle to find the right spot and time to sell. Meanwhile, residents and commuters want to avoid crowded areas. By analyzing HVV data and mobility patterns, we bridge both needs — creating valuable insights that empower smarter daily decisions for everyone.

How It Works

Using data from HVV, weather APIs, and local events, our model predicts crowd intensity across the city. With the power of data science and machine learning, we clean, process, and visualize numerical trends — giving an intelligent snapshot of city movement in real time.

Impact for Hamburg

Our project supports smarter urban living. Business owners can find the best times and places to set up shop. Citizens can plan quieter routes. Urban planners can better distribute city resources. It’s not just data — it’s insight that drives action.

Looking Ahead

We aim to extend this project into an interactive dashboard with live data visualization, offering predictive analytics accessible to everyone. The vision is a more connected, data-informed Hamburg — efficient, vibrant, and sustainable.
