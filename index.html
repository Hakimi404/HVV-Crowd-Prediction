HVV Crowd Prediction

Smarter Decisions for a Dynamic Hamburg

Our Vision

Hamburg moves fast — its streets, cyclists, and public transport form a living network of motion. Our vision is to use data from HVV, traffic lights, and health apps like cycling trackers to understand and forecast this movement. With this knowledge, we empower smarter living, smarter mobility, and smarter business choices.

Why This Project Matters

Every day, food trucks, small shops, and new startups struggle to find where people gather most. Meanwhile, residents want to avoid traffic jams and congested streets. Our model predicts crowd intensity based on real mobility and health data — bridging the gap between citizens and entrepreneurs with actionable insights.

How It Works

By combining HVV transport data, Hamburg traffic light activity, and anonymized cycling data from health applications, we built a numerical prediction model that forecasts crowd levels across time and space. With advanced data cleaning, feature engineering, and regression techniques, we turn raw signals into meaningful predictions.

Impact for Hamburg

Entrepreneurs can plan where to place their business. Commuters can choose less crowded routes. City planners can balance urban flow. Our solution strengthens the connection between technology and community — creating a healthier, more adaptive city environment.

Looking Ahead

Next, we aim to evolve this concept into a live interactive platform. Imagine an open dashboard that visualizes Hamburg’s pulse — showing real-time crowd forecasts and safe, efficient paths for everyone. That’s the vision of a truly smart Hamburg.
